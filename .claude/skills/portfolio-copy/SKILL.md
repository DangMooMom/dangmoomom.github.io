# Portfolio copy rules

Full guide (Korean, kept up to date): https://claude.ai/artifact/HErcmmdfbHJbWZNfR39MfC

## Evidence (most important)
- Only claim what was measured or confirmed. Never imply unmeasured results or causation.
- Separate intent from result. Use "would" for assumptions.
- When shortening, never drop the value users got ("so users ...").
- Do not use "+1,310%" visits.
- Do not write "company ontology". At Tridge only food product was an ontology. Use "one company model".
- Use "most companies", never a ratio, for companies that both buy and sell.

## Sentence flow (website)
- Write natural prose that a native reader takes in at once. Mix long and short sentences.
- Do not string short sentences together. Choppy text breaks the flow.
- A sentence that is not a list has at most one comma.
- Do not put several comma-joined sentences in a row. Mix in sentences without commas.
- Do not start a sentence with Yet, So, As a result, That way or On top of that. Start with the subject.
  Time openers are fine (Before full rollout, Once the tool is in daily use).
- Avoid "A, B and C" lists. They read as AI-written. Say one action instead.
- "I" at most once per block. Otherwise use the product or users as subject.
- No colons or semicolons in body text.
- Max two highlights per block. No mint (point colour) text on project pages.
- When editing, evolve the existing text. Do not rewrite it from scratch.

## Words
- UK spelling.
- Never use "module". Use the feature name, "feature", "page", "section" or "tool". Bigger units are "workflow".
- Plain words over technical titles (e.g. "Historical data in one upload").
- Say "our design lead", not "my manager".
- Name a diagram by what it actually shows. Borrow method terms (e.g. "Object map") only when they fit exactly.
  The entity, attribute and mutation diagram is "Data model".
- Keep the "Human-in-the-loop AI" chip.

## Website vs PDF
- The site shows only what each block needs. Details live in later sections.
- The summary under each project cover = a short version of the PDF Overview (Situation, Goal, Design process).
  Leave it out of style passes unless asked.
- Change Figma first when asked, then the site.
