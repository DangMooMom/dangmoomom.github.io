# Portfolio copy rules

## Evidence (most important)
- Only claim what was measured or confirmed. Never imply unmeasured results or causation.
- Separate intent from result. Use "would" for assumptions.
- When shortening, never drop the value users got ("so users ...").
- Do not use "+1,310%" visits.
- Do not write "company ontology". At Tridge only food product was an ontology.

## Style
- UK spelling.
- Key point first. Short and clear. Mix sentence lengths so it does not sound robotic.
- "I" at most once per block. Otherwise use the product or users as subject.
- No colons or semicolons in body text.
- Do not list three things with commas ("A, B and C"). Say one action instead.
- Do not chain clauses with commas. One sentence, one idea.
- Max two highlights per block.

## Words
- Never use "module". Use the feature name, "feature", "page", "section" or "tool".
- Plain words over technical titles (e.g. "Historical data in one upload").
- Say "our design lead", not "my manager".

## Website vs PDF
- The site shows only what each block needs. Details live in later sections.
- The summary under each project cover = a short version of the PDF Overview (Situation, Goal, Design process).
